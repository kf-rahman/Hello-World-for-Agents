## Purpose
Place the tool the learner is using on the map, and show that the course's
concepts carry over to other tools.

## Guide instructions
1. Go through the surfaces table from the module README at the learner's level.
2. Ask: *"Which surface are you using right now? Where is this agent actually
   running: on your machine or in the cloud?"* Confirm or correct their answer.
3. Ask them to name **two things from Modules 1 to 4 that would work the same
   way on a different surface**, and **one thing that would be different**.

## Learner steps
1. Identify the surface they're on.
2. Name two things that transfer and one that doesn't.

## Completion check
The learner correctly identified their surface and named two transferable
concepts (for example prompting, memory files, skills, MCP) and one difference
(for example where it runs, file access, how approvals work, or synchronous vs
asynchronous work).

## Hints
1. Think about what you *wrote* in Modules 1 to 4 (prompts, memory files,
   skills). Are those tied to one tool?

## Takeaway
Learn the concepts once and they apply in every tool. The surface decides
where the work happens and how involved you are, not how you think about the
task.
