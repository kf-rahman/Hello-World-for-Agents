## Purpose
Practice choosing a surface for a job.

## Guide instructions
Give the learner four scenarios matched to their persona. For each, they pick a
surface and give a one-line reason. Accept any well-reasoned answer, then share
the typical choice.

**Engineer scenarios**
1. Fix a typo in 20 documentation files while you're in a meeting.
2. Carefully refactor a core module you know well, reviewing each change.
3. Every new pull request should get an automatic first-pass review.
4. Investigate a failing build on your laptop that depends on local services.

**Business scenarios**
1. Summarize this week's customer feedback from a shared drive into a one-page brief.
2. Clean up a 5,000-row spreadsheet and produce a monthly chart.
3. Every Monday, draft a status update from the team's tracker.
4. Brainstorm the structure of a proposal before writing it.

Typical choices: engineer: cloud, IDE, automation, CLI. Business: chat or
desktop app with a connector, a CLI or desktop agent working on local files,
automation or a scheduled task, chat.

## Learner steps
1. Pick a surface for each scenario and give a reason.

## Completion check
The learner gave a reason for each of the four choices, and at least three of
them fit the scenario.

## Hints
1. Ask three questions: where are the files, where should it run, and do I need
   to watch it?

## Takeaway
Choose based on where the work is and how involved you want to be. The brand
matters less than the surface.
