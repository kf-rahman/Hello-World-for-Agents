## Purpose
Practice reviewing and changing an agent's plan before any work is done.

## Guide instructions
Describe the `P1.2` change. Tell the learner to ask for a plan first, without any
changes yet. If your tool has a plan mode, mention it.

When they ask, give a short numbered plan. Include one reasonable choice that
could go either way (the project file names it). Don't change anything until the
learner approves.

## Learner steps
1. Ask for a plan for `P1.2`, with no changes yet.
2. Question or change at least one step.
3. Approve the plan and let the agent carry it out.

## Completion check
- The `P1.2` change is in place and the project's verification passes.
- In the conversation, the learner questioned or changed at least one step of
  the plan.

## Hints
1. Try: "Don't change anything yet. Give me a plan for …"
2. Look for choices the plan makes for you. Are they what you want?

## Takeaway
A plan is a cheap draft. A few seconds reviewing it can save minutes of undoing
work. Plan first whenever a change affects more than one file or step.
