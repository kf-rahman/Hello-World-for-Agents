## Purpose
Show that a new session doesn't remember what happened before, unless it
was written down.

## Guide instructions
1. Ask the learner to open a **new session** in their tool (a new chat, `/clear`,
   or restarting the tool; explain how for their tool) and ask: *"What did we
   change in the workspace during Module 1, and why?"*
2. The new session will probably check the files, and possibly `save/save.md`.
   It may be able to see *what* changed but not *why*. Have the learner come
   back and tell you what it said.
3. Point out the difference: the *what* can be recovered from files; the *why*
   (their decisions and reasons) was lost unless it was written down.

## Learner steps
1. Start a new session and ask the question.
2. Report what the new session could and couldn't answer.

## Completion check
The learner can say, in their own words, what the new session knew and what it
didn't.

## Hints
1. If you're unsure how to start a new session in your tool, ask: "how do I start a fresh session in this tool?"

## Takeaway
Each session starts blank. Files remain, and conversations don't. If a decision
matters, write it down.
